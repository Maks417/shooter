## Owner direction — 2026-11-14

**Latest quality goal:** the finished design should be **as good as Battlefield V, Bodycam and Warface**. [Visual milestones VD0–VD9](design-milestones.md) are the canonical execution and review plan, superseding the earlier five-stage grouping. The current UI/staging implementation is a partial VD7 foundation; no reference-quality milestone is accepted. VD1 must prove a representative art slice within the existing procedural and reference-performance constraints before broad production.

The owner subsequently supplied six Battlefield screenshots and clarified that the menu and combat interface must feel like a finished military FPS. The flat contour-only menu is superseded by a scene-led layout: a large original procedural staging scene, compact left navigation and two rendered operation cards. Combat uses a compact compass/score strip, a north-up friendly map with temporary enemy gunfire reveal, squad status, small ammo/reload readouts and a bounded elimination feed. The supplied screenshots are composition references, not imported backgrounds or shipping art.

The owner requested the complete visual design and selected implementation in Unity, in stages beginning with menus and HUD. Reference games: Battlefield, Call of Duty, Warface and Bodycam. These are mood and hierarchy references, never source assets or a promise of matching their rendering cost or fidelity. Consulted publisher/developer pages: [Battlefield media](https://www.ea.com/games/battlefield/battlefield-6/features/bf6-media-hub), [Call of Duty](https://www.callofduty.com/en/blackops7/multiplayer), [Warface](https://steamcommunity.com/app/291480/announcements/) and [Bodycam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2406770/Bodycam/).

The original direction combines military restraint, large clear headings, tactile equipment and natural light. Use charcoal/graphite surfaces, ivory text, sand amber selection and labeled cyan/coral team identity. Bodycam is a reference for grounded light and physical presence; aggressive distortion, obligatory shake and unreadable darkness are not requirements. Keep accessibility preferences and the 720p Low target.

The milestone plan covers art direction/feasibility, soldiers, hands/weapons, procedural motion, both environments, lighting/materials/effects, final UI, measured scalability and comparative installer acceptance. Its per-stage deliverables and exits live once in [design-milestones.md](design-milestones.md). The working [VD0 art brief](visual-art-brief.md) holds the comparison register, gap inventory and presentation ownership; it stays draft until the owner approves exact Bodycam/Warface frames. Earlier T074 software and retained captures remain valid evidence of implementation; P05 and final visual/device review remain open.

No store, progression, matchmaking or online lobby is introduced. The lobby is local match setup with one human plus bots. The full visual overhaul is not complete when stage 1 ships.

## Implemented shell and HUD

`TacticalUi` draws solid rectangles, lines and an analytic decorative contour field using the engine white texture plus the ADR-066 Barlow Condensed UI typeface bank (engine IMGUI font fallback); it imports no image sprites or scene art and creates no surface-texture caches. Its coordinate space applies the persisted UI scale once; layouts use the actual logical display bounds and a 5% safe inset. The contour field is decoration, not a map preview. Main/setup/loading are full-screen; pause and confirmations cover the paused world with a near-opaque dark scrim. Loading/boot use a shared operations-insertion briefing (`DrawInsertionScreen`): accent-rail plate, large map name, rules line, phase strip, optional packaged map-card media, and a bracketed dual-track indeterminate activity bar—never a guessed completion percentage. Reduced camera motion freezes the activity segment to a solid accent underline.

For the scene-led revision, `ProductMenuScene` reads both approved Low visual packages through the optional pure-byte `IProductMenuVisualSource` seam on the real Bootstrap backend. It instantiates a bounded numerical staging layout from the verified unit-box mesh and three cosmetic copies of the existing packaged soldier/rifle. All LODs already belong to each visual package; the menu uses LOD0 independently of gameplay's applied LOD. There is no soldier-generator or clearance iteration. The scene has no colliders, navigation, live participants or fake gameplay adapters. Render-only layer 12 is excluded from the gameplay camera and sun. A 1280×720 render target updates at most 30 Hz only while the front end owns it; two 384×216 cards render the actual packaged map-solid meshes once. RenderSettings changes are scoped and restored around offscreen draws, and all menu objects/resources are owned by one shell child. Reduced motion stops cosmetic dust movement; entering loading/play/pause disables the staging scene. Missing verified bytes expose a repair message, without generated-content fallback.

The tactical map projects verified collision-box footprints, the local Fill-based self marker, current allied snapshots, and temporary enemy gunfire reveals within 42 metres. Enemy blips appear only after authority `ShotAccepted` from the opposing team and expire after a short hold; silent enemies stay hidden. It never streams live enemy poses, changes sensing, or makes physics queries ([ADR-058](decisions.md#adr-058--accepted-minimap-self-marker-and-temporary-gunfire-reveal)). Recent elimination rows consume observed authority Death events with a fixed 32-event dedupe ring and four expiring display rows; they are not a match-history database. Each row draws killer and victim names with a procedural Fill-based weapon silhouette (and a headshot mark when the fatal `DamageApplied` region is head) correlated from the same leased tick batch—no imported icons and no DeathPayload contract change. First-person meshes are now assigned on the initial packaged bind, before slot switching. The first active clock sample rebases across loading, so visual binding is not charged as combat time; actual active-frame overload still fails. IMGUI Escape and applied-input Escape share a once-per-frame guard. Text tints compensate the locked Linear-color IMGUI font path so the intended sRGB palette survives rendering.

Buttons use 18 logical-pixel text and visible focus/selection bars. Tab/Shift-Tab and up/down traverse enabled controls; Enter/Space select and left/right adjust focused sliders. Escape retains its reserved recovery role. Settings groups are Audio, Video, Controls and Accessibility, with a scrolling content region and fixed Apply/Cancel/Defaults/Back actions; existing schema-2 storage, conflicts and 15-second Keep/Revert behavior remain authoritative. Binding capture ignores its initiating frame.

Team tables place six rows per side with aligned Score/kills/deaths columns and a highlighted YOU row. The combat HUD uses opaque compact backing plates, a centered team score and minutes/seconds clock, lower-corner health/ammo, event/tick-driven reload progress, empty-ammo guidance, a death/authority-respawn notice, and ADR-059/ADR-073 procedural score badges (upper kill ribbon with life-scoped ENEMY KILLED / DOUBLE / TRIPLE / MULTI labels, mid-screen feed including ASSIST and extra HEADSHOT, ammo +N popup) drawn with fills only—no imported ribbon art. ADS hides the analytic hip reticle so the weapon sight remains visible. HUD/preferences remain read-only consumers of gameplay.

## Shared visual principles

The new [game shell](features/game-shell.md) specifies actual menu/setup/loading/pause/settings/scoreboard/results behavior and working persistence/device controls for M10. Existing HudLayout is a pure helper; its test is not interactive UI evidence. The tokens below guide `Presentation/Session/TacticalUi.cs`; they stay subject to named validation in the real 720p player, including the 12-row tables and 150% scale. No image preview requirement is introduced for map selection.

Grounded, functional, readable modern military training environment. Favor believable proportions, physical material scale, restrained wear and clear player silhouettes over noisy detail. Fictional insignia generated from lines/polygons, no imported logos. Realism review uses silhouette, material response, spatial scale and motion separately; a complex shader cannot compensate for implausible proportions.

| UI token | Value | Use |
|---|---|---|
| surface | #161C20 | Menus and diagnostics |
| text | #F1F3EF | Primary text |
| muted | #ADB7B7 | Secondary text |
| focus | #E6C56A | Keyboard/controller focus |
| danger | #E47B70 | Damage/state, always paired with shape/text |
| ally | #7BC5D1 | Friendly marker with icon/label |

Implement UI with layout rectangles, lines and generated vector/mesh reticles; no image sprites. **Shape layer (ADR-084):** plates, buttons, cards, sliders, toggles, reticle arms, icons and the kill-ribbon medal are analytic SDF shapes (`Shooter/UiSdf`) with tokens from `UiShapeParams`: plate radius 4, button 3, card 6, chip 2, HUD plate 3; soft shadow 3 px down / 10 px soft at 38%; focus glow 8 px at 28%. Hover/focus/selection ease through `UiMotion` (snap under reduced motion; no glint and softer glow under reduced flash). **Icons (ADR-085):** every glyph comes from the generated SDF atlas (`IconLibrary` names); weapon icons are silhouettes traced from the packaged weapon meshes. Faction emblems differ by shape (ally shield, enemy hexagon) as well as cyan/coral. Icons take the same fill/shadow/glow styling as shapes; never draw an imported or painted icon. **Typography (ADR-066):** Barlow Condensed (OFL) — Regular for body/captions, SemiBold for buttons, Bold for display titles (≥22 px). Loaded from `Presentation/Fonts/Resources`; engine IMGUI font remains the load fallback. Closure token `ui-typeface-bank-v1`. At 720p output, body 18 px, key ammo 28 px, titles 32–56 px, minimum interactive target 32×32 px, 4/8/12/16/24 spacing. UI scale 100–150%, safe area 5%; ensure important HUD does not collide at maximum scale. Minimum text/background contrast 4.5:1; validate actual colors rather than assuming table passes all combinations. Player-facing copy stays short military FPS language (`ShellUiCopy` / `InsertionScreenCopy`); no schema/version jargon in chrome.

Components: reticle with optional center dot and spread ring, ammo/action indicator, health, objective text, pause/settings, quality selector and benchmark report. Reload feedback includes remaining action time; network reconnect/loading has plain text and no hidden technical stack traces. Performance overlay is opt-in developer mode, shows frame time rather than just FPS.

Accessibility: independent mouse sensitivity, field-of-view range 70–100 vertical degrees with explicit label, reduced camera motion/flash, remappable controls, subtitles for objective cues, color-independent team indicators and readable fonts. Choose and test FOV conversion once; rifle ADS uses analytic screen-space PiP (blurred periphery + digital lens zoom), still no recursive render-to-texture optics ([ADR-065](decisions.md#adr-065--accepted-screen-space-pip-rifle-scope-blurred-periphery)). All settings persist versioned user configuration outside generated content.

Acceptance: 720p screenshots of HUD/menu/loading and maximum scale readable with no clipping; color contrast and keyboard navigation verified; reticle ADS and world collision visibility checks pass; UI uses no prohibited visual assets. Visual snapshots are reviewed with a named baseline build and seed, not copied external imagery as source assets.


## ADR-087 combat HUD tokens

Damage direction uses a 118 logical-pixel radius, 64-pixel arc length and 5-pixel stroke. Reduced flash halves arc alpha. Ammo retains numeric counts and adds up to 30 cartridge glyphs; oversized magazines use a compact bar. Reload is a 20-pixel ring from the existing authority-tick phase. Allies/self use north-oriented arrow glyphs rotated by copied yaw; enemy gunfire uses the hollow diamond under ADR-058 expiry/range rules. A head hit uses a gold marker 1.25 times the ordinary size; the 0.14-second pop snaps to final size under reduced motion. Low-health vignette strength stays within the existing maximum; the slow pulse stops under either reduced flash or reduced motion. Elimination names have faint team plates, a 24-pixel entry slide and smoothly following rows; reduced motion fixes positions immediately. These are presentation tokens, not visual acceptance.


## Full roadmap visual recipes — ADR-088 through ADR-094

Map surfaces opt into seeded concrete/plaster, chipped coated steel, oxide, asphalt, gravel and corrugation from `material.noise` 1.0.0. Physical repeat is 2 m, with 128 px Low/Balanced and 256 px High; uniform camo repeats every 0.25 m. Keep grime subordinate to structural mass and readable combat silhouettes. Surface dirt is concentrated low on walls and in occluded regions.

Compound uses morning haze, Depot overcast and Oil Depot a low golden-hour sun; clouds and reflected sky come from the same original numeric cube recipe. Per-map grade uses bounded gain/lift/contrast/saturation integers; Low stays LDR with ColorAdjustments only. Contact shadows and sampled body visibility ground the soldiers without changing collision or hit volumes.

Paint uses numeric stencil marks, warning stripes, unit chevrons and bounded stains. Combat FX use five packaged 8x8 FBm/curl flipbooks and a capped particle pool; Low reduces count and skips depth softening. Reduced flash suppresses muzzle, tracer and explosion fire/flash; reduced motion suppresses camera shake.

The 1.0.3 uniform adds contoured carrier/pouch construction, sleeve detail, grouped numeric digits, faction armbands and shoulder patches from the existing emblem recipes. Legacy 1.0.2 remains reproducible. The ADR-094 bounded correction failed; [ADR-095](decisions.md#adr-095--accepted-structural-weapon-from-eye-shooting-posture-p05-reopening) then made the structural shooting posture the product default, resolving the numeric P05 gate (visual review pending). None of these changes establishes reference-game parity or visual acceptance.
