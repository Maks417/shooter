# M1 weapon recipe baseline v1

Initial numeric construction and socket assignment for P03, 2026-11-12. This is an additive generator/recipe registry, not a C0 DTO or SGAF layout change. See [geometry/weapons](features/geometry-weapons.md), [format v1](content-format.md) and [M1 handoff](milestones/m1-procedural-feasibility/handoff.md). Visual review and final gameplay/view integration remain required; no combat mechanics are implemented by these generators.

Coordinates are meters internally, integer millimeters in recipes. +Z points forward, +Y up, +X right. Weapon origin is the right-hand grip. Rigid sockets use unit quaternion rotations and no scale; initial rotations are identity. Stable semantic names: muzzle, chamber-ejection, magazine-grip, left-hand-grip, right-hand-grip, shoulder, sight-eye, attachment-rail. Rifle requires all eight; pistol requires all except shoulder. Socket transforms derive from the numeric construction, remain identical for all four LODs and all visual profiles, and do not consume cosmetic RNG.

Initial rifle overall length is 960 mm (rear Z=-320, muzzle Z=640), overall height about 300 mm and main width about 80 mm. Main barrel/muzzle center is (0,80,640) mm. Receiver/handguard/stock/grip/magazine/sights/trigger-guard/bolt/charging-handle are constructed closed primitive components, with bounded cavities where used. Required sockets in mm: muzzle=(0,80,640), chamber-ejection=(35,85,100), magazine-grip=(0,-75,65), left-hand-grip=(0,10,340), right-hand-grip=(0,0,0), shoulder=(0,60,-320), sight-eye=(0,115,-40), attachment-rail=(0,120,220).

Initial pistol length is 210 mm (rear Z=-80, muzzle Z=130), overall height about 160 mm and width about 36 mm. Required sockets in mm: muzzle=(0,70,130), chamber-ejection=(16,75,65), magazine-grip=(0,-70,-20), left-hand-grip=(-22,0,-5), right-hand-grip=(0,0,0), sight-eye=(0,100,-70), attachment-rail=(0,20,70). Construct frame/slide/barrel/grip/magazine/sights/trigger guard. No shoulder socket is fabricated for a handgun.

These are fictional, unbranded reference proportions, not copied model vertices or licensed weapon dimensions. Four LOD ceilings are rifle 18000/8000/3000/800 triangles and pistol 8000/4000/1500/400. Counts are ceilings, not minimum detail requirements. Rule-derived simplification removes bevels/small mechanical components and lowers radial segments; it does not move gameplay sockets or use render LODs for collision. Collision proxies are generated independently from the same mechanical dimensions. Mesh topology validation welds coincident positions for solid-edge manifold checks while preserving rendering seams/hard normals/UVs. No seed changes mechanical dimensions; optional wear uses independent named streams.

The P03 weapon API exposes immutable source/build/socket DTOs outside C0. Mesh/collision/material payloads use SGAF v1 exclusively; no private on-disk socket codec is introduced. Numeric sockets are reported with source/generator versions and consumed by later shared gameplay/view pose integration. Schema versions/generator IDs are explicitly registered by the integrator before compiler composition. Previous P02 collision diagnostic content remains supported unchanged.

## geometry.weapon 1.1.0 parametric rifle parts (ADR-082)

1.1.0 recipes replace `silhouette` with `parameters.parts`. A rifle states `handguardFrontMm` (300–485), `barrelFrontMm` (handguard + 40 to 640), `topRailTeeth` (4–15, ending before the handguard front), `gasBlockZMm` (300 to barrel − 100), `gasTubeLengthMm` (40–160), `gasTubeZMm` (the tube fits between 225 mm and the gas block), `frontSightZMm` (handguard − 100 to barrel − 50), `stock` (`fixed`/`none`) and `optic` (`compact`/`none`). A pistol states `parts: {}`. Rail, under-rail and forward-fastener counts stop at the handguard front.

The 1.0.5 silhouettes are presets: `standard` = 485/640/15/480/400/120/530 with stock and optic, and `carbine` = 380/520/10/360/300/80/400 with stock and optic. A 1.1.0 recipe with those values builds byte-identical LODs, magazine, cycle part, collision and sockets. Sockets, collision, magazine, cycle part and the construction envelope stay on the numeric baseline above for every part combination. Meshes are fingerprinted with the recipe's own generator version. Shipped recipes remain on 1.0.5.

## geometry.weapon 1.2.0 named archetypes (ADR-102)

1.2.0 recipes state `parameters.kind` as one of `ak`, `ar`, `bolt`, `dmr`, `pistol-eagle` or `pistol-striker`, and `parameters.parts` as `barrelFrontMm`, `furniture` (`black`, `gold`, `polymer`, `wood`), `muzzle` (`birdcage`, `brake`, `flash`, `none`, `slant`), `optic` (`irons`, `scope2`, `scope4`, `scope8`) and `stock` (`chassis`, `fixed`, `folding`, `none`, `wood`). Barrel front ranges are ak 360–600, ar 420–780, dmr 480–760, bolt 700–960, pistol-striker 110–140 and pistol-eagle 150–200 mm. Pistols take `irons` and no stock; long guns need a stock. Each kind has its own sockets, collision, magazine, cycle part and envelope (front = barrel + muzzle device + 12 mm). Material slots 5 (wood) and 6 (gold) are used only when the furniture asks for them. 1.0.5 and 1.1.0 recipes and their output are unchanged; `docs/feature-fixtures/rifle-1.2.0.json` is the AK-47 recipe.

## geometry.weapon 1.4.0 bare-rail long guns (ADR-107)

1.4.0 takes the 1.2.0/1.3.0 parameters and builds the 1.3.0 profile bodies, with one change: long guns state `optic: "mount"` and carry no optic of their own (pistols keep `irons`). `mount` is rejected before 1.4.0, and 1.4.0 long guns accept nothing else. The body's `sight-eye` socket is its iron-sight line (AK and AR keep their 1.3.0 irons line; DMR and bolt sight 18 mm above their top rails); every other socket, the collision, magazine, cycle part and envelope are unchanged. Sights (`irons`, `collimator`, `scope2`, `scope4`, `scope8`) are separate `geometry.weapon-sight` meshes (1.1.0 since ADR-115) built per archetype rail in the gun's weapon space, inside the gun's envelope widened to 0.27 m tall; see [weapons](features/geometry-weapons.md#rail-mounted-sights-adr-107). `docs/feature-fixtures/rifle-1.4.0.json` is the M16 recipe.

## geometry.weapon 1.5.0 Mk 16, M870 and the carbine stock (ADR-118)

1.5.0 builds every 1.4.0 recipe byte-identically and adds two long-gun kinds and one stock, all rejected below 1.5.0. `scar` (Mk 16, barrel 480–680 mm) is a monolithic railed upper over a polymer lower with a left-side reciprocating charging handle (the cycle part), a STANAG magazine and a side-folding stock; its butt and `shoulder` socket sit 415 mm behind the grip. `pump` (M870, barrel 560–760 mm) is a milled receiver with a short top rail, a vent-ribbed barrel with a bead, a magazine tube and a pistol-grip butt; its cycle part is the pump forend and its magazine part is one 12-gauge shell lying in the loading port. Stock `collapsible` (only `ar` and `scar`) is a buffer tube with a sliding carbine stock, butt and `shoulder` socket 360 mm behind the grip; on `ar` it also gives the M4 carbine handguard ending 30 mm behind the front sight base. Both new kinds take rail-mounted sights: Mk 16 irons are the DMR flip-up pair, M870 irons are a ghost ring on the receiver rail and a blade clamped on the barrel, both 18 mm above the rail. `docs/feature-fixtures/rifle-1.5.0.json` is the Mk 16 recipe.
